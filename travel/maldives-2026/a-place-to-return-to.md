# A Place to Return To

Our October 2026 holiday at Constance Moofushi in the Maldives was our third visit to "The Jewel Island", a little island in the South Ari Atoll. We had first visited in 2015, returned in 2018, and eleven years after that first encounter found ourselves once again surrounded by its brilliant white beaches, turquoise lagoons, coconut palms and extraordinary Indian Ocean scenery.

Over the years, we have accumulated a substantial collection of photographs recording not only the island's remarkable beauty, but also its wildlife, familiar landmarks, quieter corners and the little details that make it such a distinctive place.

_A Place to Return To_ brings together selected photographs from our 2015 and 2026 visits into a short film.

Unlike _Beneath the Surface_, which explores the extraordinary marine life of the surrounding reefs, this film remains above the water, following the island's beaches and pathways, villas, birdlife and other inhabitants and the everyday pleasures of island life. There are familiar locations revisited eleven years apart, unexpected encounters with wildlife, tropical storms, magnificent skies and, inevitably, a few moments devoted to the demanding business of doing absolutely nothing!

The film begins with the seaplane journey across the Maldivian atolls and our arrival at Moofushi, before exploring the island's landscapes, turquoise lagoon, beaches and familiar landmarks. Along the way, we encounter some of the wildlife that shares this tiny island with its human visitors, and enjoy its quieter corners, familiar haunts and the everyday pleasures of island life.

Changing light, dramatic tropical skies and the extraordinary colours of the Indian Ocean provide a backdrop throughout and, hopefully, the images convey something of what makes Moofushi such a special place for us, and why, having discovered it all those years ago, we keep wanting to return.
