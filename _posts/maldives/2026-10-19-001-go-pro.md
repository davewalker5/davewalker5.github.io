We've been spending a fair bit of time snorkelling here in the Maldives, recording the extraordinary marine life around the island. Turtles, reef mantas, sharks, countless colourful fish — there's certainly no shortage of subjects!

And all of it has been filmed using two rather elderly GoPro cameras.

One is a HERO4 Silver, the other an original HERO. Both models were released in 2014, and we bought them for our first visit to the Maldives in 2015.

Which means they're now twelve years old.

Neither has image stabilisation, one doesn't even have a screen on the back, and their video capabilities are, to say the least, modest by modern standards. But they still work beautifully, and the footage we've brought back from the reef has been a delight.

I briefly considered replacing the older one before this trip, largely because it lacks that rear screen. But, as it turns out, when you're floating around in the ocean trying to keep yourself steady while a turtle grazes on the reef below, carefully composing a shot on a tiny screen isn't particularly realistic anyway. It's mostly a case of pointing the camera in roughly the right direction and hoping for the best!

And the results have been wonderful.

There's something quietly pleasing about discovering that a piece of technology bought for a holiday eleven years ago is still perfectly capable of capturing the magic of the same place today.

No upgrades, no replacements. Just two old cameras, still doing their thing.

And long may they continue.
