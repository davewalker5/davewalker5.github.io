ReefFrame, FishTrack & ReefTag

Three open-source tools for exploring underwater footage and recording marine wildlife

These tools grew out of filming marine wildlife while snorkelling in the Maldives, and wondering what more I could learn from the footage afterwards.

ReefFrame helps clarify and enhance underwater video, prepare clips and extract still images. FishTrack is a more experimental companion, using computer vision and AI-based tracking to find and follow fish as they move through the footage.

ReefTag brings the footage into the Field Notes natural-history recording workflow: Identifying and cataloguing species seen in the images, retaining links to the source footage, and transferring observation records into Nature Recorder. Together, the tools offer different ways to explore a wildlife encounter — from improving the images to recording what was seen.

All three applications are open source, with source code, documentation and instructions available on GitHub.
