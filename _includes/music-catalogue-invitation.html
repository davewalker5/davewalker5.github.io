Looking beyond individual records to discover the patterns within a collection

I wrote my own Music Catalogue application to manage my record collection, recording not only the usual catalogue information but also the stylistic characteristics of the music itself.

Through reporting and visualisation, the application allows me to explore the collection from different perspectives — examining genres, energy, intimacy, warmth, collecting depth and buying patterns, and occasionally discovering things about my musical interests that I hadn't consciously recognised.
