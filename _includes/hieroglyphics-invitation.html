Hieroglyphics Transliterator

Exploring the signs, sounds and conventions of ancient Egyptian writing through interactive experimentation.

Developed for enjoyment and inspired by a personal interest in Egyptology, the Hieroglyphics Transliterator provides an accessible introduction to some of the fascinating characteristics of ancient Egyptian writing.

Rather than attempting to translate modern text into ancient Egyptian, the project uses a simplified transliteration approach as a starting point for exploration and allows users to render familiar words and phrases using selected hieroglyphic signs, explore their approximate phonetic associations and experiment with different reading directions.

The complete source code, documentation and instructions for running the application are available in the GitHub repository.
