Return to Moofushi, 2026

Field notes from a small island in the Indian Ocean

A return to the Maldives becomes an exploration of life on and around a small coral island — from the extraordinary wildlife of its reefs to the quieter encounters along its shores.

Combining personal observations, photographs, and scientific curiosity, this journal follows two weeks at Moofushi and the questions, discoveries, and reflections that grew from them.
