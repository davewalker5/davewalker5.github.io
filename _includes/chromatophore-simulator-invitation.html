Chromatophore Simulator

Exploring cephalopod-inspired chromatophores, dynamic skin patterning, and emergent visual displays

The skin patterns shown throughout these investigations were generated using a graphical simulation of thousands of small, independently controlled pigment cells inspired by cephalopod chromatophores.

By varying cell expansion and contraction, response timing, and the patterns of activation across the field, the same underlying population can produce spots, bands, mottles, travelling waves, pulses, and other changing displays.

The project is intended not as a detailed reconstruction of cephalopod physiology, but as an experiment in computational natural history: exploring how complex and coordinated surface patterns can emerge from the behaviour of many comparatively simple individual elements.

The Python application, source code, documentation, and instructions for running the simulator are provided in the GitHub repository. A browser-based version is also available as an interactive explorer.
