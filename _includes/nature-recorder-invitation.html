Nature Recorder

Nature Recorder is a natural-history recording application developed to capture, organise, and explore observations of the natural world.

By recording species encounters alongside their dates and locations, individual observations accumulate into a structured dataset through which patterns of species richness, geographical distribution, and seasonal occurrence can be explored.

The project is intended not simply as a wildlife recording system, but as a means of transforming everyday encounters with nature into a cumulative body of observational evidence, supporting a deeper understanding of the biodiversity around us.

The complete source code, documentation and instructions for running the application are available in the GitHub repository.
