From Encounter to Evidence

Transforming recreational underwater footage into structured natural history observations

Beginning with four snorkelling excursions in the Maldives and approximately 50 Gb of underwater footage, this case study explores how curiosity led to the development of specialist software for video enhancement, animal tracking and species identification, complementing an established natural history recording and analysis system.

The study examines how recreational wildlife encounters can become structured, traceable and analysable observations, while recognising the limitations of opportunistic recording and considering its wider relevance to natural history and citizen science.
