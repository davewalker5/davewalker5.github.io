ReefFrame & FishTrack

Two complementary tools for exploring underwater video

ReefFrame and FishTrack grew out of my experiences filming marine wildlife in the Maldives, and a curiosity about what more could be discovered in the resulting footage.

ReefFrame provides a visual interface for clarifying and enhancing video recordings, extracting still images and preparing clips. FishTrack takes the next step, using computer vision and AI-based techniques to identify and follow fish as they move through the footage.

Both applications are open source, with their source code, documentation and instructions available on GitHub.
