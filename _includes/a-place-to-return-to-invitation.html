A Place to Return To

Constance Moofushi — an island remembered across eleven years

Some places leave an impression that endures long after the journey home. For me, Constance Moofushi in the Maldives is one of those places.

This photographic film brings together images taken eleven years apart, exploring the island's extraordinary landscapes, wildlife, everyday life and changing skies. It celebrates both the beauty of Moofushi and the memories accumulated through successive visits.

A personal reflection on a remarkable little island, the passage of time, and the enduring pleasure of returning somewhere that continues to inspire wonder.
