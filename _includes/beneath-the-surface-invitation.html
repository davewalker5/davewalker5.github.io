Beneath the Surface

Exploring the marine wildlife of South Ari Atoll, Maldives

Beneath the turquoise waters of the Maldives lies an extraordinary world of coral reefs, colourful fish and magnificent marine animals.

Filmed during snorkelling excursions in October 2026, this short film brings together encounters with reef fish, hawksbill turtles, reef manta rays and tawny nurse sharks, celebrating the beauty and diversity of the underwater world.

More than a record of the animals encountered, the film is a celebration of the wonder of sharing their world, however briefly, and of the experiences that inspire curiosity, appreciation and conservation.
