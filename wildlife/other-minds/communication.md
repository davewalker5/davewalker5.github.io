## Communication and Expression

Cephalopods also possess extraordinarily sophisticated systems of visual display. Chromatophores, iridophores and leucophores allow rapid changes of colour and pattern used for camouflage, threat displays and signalling.

These systems illustrate how complex communication can evolve from mechanisms that originally served other functions. Increasingly elaborate signals can develop without becoming anything resembling human language.

Again, the important lesson is not to ask how closely cephalopod communication approximates ours, but to understand it on its own evolutionary terms.
